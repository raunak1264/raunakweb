About Me

Who Am I?

I am Raunak Kapoor, a highly experienced ServiceNow Architect with over 12 years in designing, implementing, and managing ServiceNow ITOM, ITSM, and CMDB solutions. I specialise in ServiceNow development, scripting, integrations, and automation, with a strong focus on optimising IT processes, enhancing platform performance, and driving digital transformation.

My expertise spans architecting Discovery, Event Management, and Service Mapping solutions; managing MID Servers and Agent Client Collector (ACC); integrating CyberArk for secure credential management; implementing SSH, SNMP, and WMI protocols for accurate asset discovery; and applying IRE rules to enforce CMDB data consistency. I have built REST API integrations, developed custom Service Portal solutions using JavaScript, HTML, CSS, and React, and led ServiceNow instance upgrades and performance optimisations.

Adept at architecting scalable solutions and leading development teams, I am passionate about delivering high-quality ServiceNow architectures that enhance service visibility, security, and business value.
